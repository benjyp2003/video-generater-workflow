# Channel style guide

Used as context for every script prompt. Edit freely; it is the "voice" of the channel.

## Audience
Beginners, IT students, junior engineers. Assume they can open a terminal; assume nothing else.

## Voice
- Calm, direct, friendly. Second person ("you type…").
- One idea per sentence. Short sentences for narration (TTS reads them better).
- Define every term the first time it appears, in plain words.
- One concrete analogy per major section, and say where the analogy breaks.
- Name misconceptions explicitly and correct them.

## Accuracy rules
- Every factual statement must be backed by a claim in `research.md` (`[CLAIM] C#`).
- Say which versions you used. Say "typically" or "by default" when behavior is configurable.
- Never present a command you haven't run in the demo sandbox.

## Banned
- Hype words: "revolutionary", "game-changer", "insane", "secret trick".
- Clickbait that the video doesn't pay off.
- Real credentials, hostnames, usernames, IPs or account IDs on screen.

## Structure defaults
- Hook within 15 seconds: the question the video answers.
- Recap at the end: 3 bullet points max.
- End with one concrete next step (next video or something to try).

## Glossary (spelling used in captions)
containerd, runc, dockerd, cgroups, namespaces, OCI, Kubernetes, kubectl, Nginx, systemd
